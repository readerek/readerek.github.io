ABOUT ME

From tactile perception
to reliable manipulation.

I am Keyu Wang at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on tactile sensing, dexterous manipulation, and robot learning. I aim to help robots understand objects through touch and act reliably during complex physical interactions.

My work spans tactile simulation and representation learning, multimodal manipulation policies, deformable object grasping, and reinforcement learning for real-time communication.
